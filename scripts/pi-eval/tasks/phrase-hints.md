OWNER RULE: free-form chat means the owner says what they want in ANY words and the LLM interpreter picks the operation. No magic words/phrases; never tell the owner to say a particular sentence; no keyword scans of owner text to decide intent. ID-invention guards (foreignValue/wholeWord) are fine. Proposals (mode "propose") state the consequence plainly and the owner's confirmation of the shown proposal is the consent. Text from agents/records shown to the owner is escaped (short()/quotedText() in chat/commands.ts, quoted() in status/explain.ts); never double-escape.
